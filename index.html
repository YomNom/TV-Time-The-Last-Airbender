Show Summary

Avatar: The Last Airbender is an western animated show that takes place in a world where people can manipulate the four elements: water, earth, fire, and air. It aired on Nickelodeon from February 21, 2005 - July 19, 2008 and contains three seasons with a total of 61 episodes. The main protagonist, Aang, is the last surviving airbender or air manipulator and has undertaken the role of the Avatar, the only person who can master all four elements and maintain balance in the world. The world is divided into four nations based on the elements: the Water Tribes, the Earth Kingdom, the Fire Nation, and the Air Nomads. Aang and his friends venture on a journey for Aang to master all four elements in order to defeat the Fire Nation, which has launched a war to conquer the world.
